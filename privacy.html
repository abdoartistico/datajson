Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Golazona ("we", "our", or "the app") is a live football scores application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Favorite leagues and teams - Stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or sync your favorites to any server.
  • Subscription status - If you purchase Golazona Pro, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to verify your subscription status. We do not store your payment details.

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data - We track basic usage metrics such as app open count to improve the user experience (e.g., determining when to show a review prompt). This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising data - For free users, we display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Crash and performance data - We may collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect your location data.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob - Advertising. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store - In-app purchases and app distribution. Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

All user preferences (favorites, theme settings, app open count) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. No personal data is transmitted to our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Data Retention

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. Subscription data managed by Apple and RevenueCat is subject to their respective retention policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device's privacy settings (Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising).
  • Manage or cancel your subscription through the App Store.

8. Ad-Free Experience

Golazona Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience. When you subscribe, no advertising SDKs collect data during your usage of the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store.
