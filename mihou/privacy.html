Mihou Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Mihou is a reader app for comics and manga. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your library, reading progress, settings, downloaded chapters and backups are stored only on your device. We do not have access to them. Deleting the app removes them.

Content sources you add

Mihou does not provide any content. When you add a source or open a title, the app connects directly from your device to that source's website. Those websites receive your IP address and normal web request information, and they are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not see or store this traffic.

App configuration

When it starts, the app downloads a small settings file from GitHub (github.com). GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving that file. No personal information is sent.

Purchases

Mihou Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your purchase is active.

Advertising

The free version of Mihou may show ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect device information such as your IP address, device model and an advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. Where required, the app asks for your consent first, and on iOS your advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Mihou Pro removes all ads.

Analytics

Mihou does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

Mihou is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because your data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing titles from your library or deleting the app. You can withdraw ad tracking consent in iPhone Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support link on Mihou's App Store page.
