Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

LibG is a reader for books in the public domain. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: LibG has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of our own. Your library, your reading position and your downloaded files stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information ourselves. LibG does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and there is no account to create.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is written to your device's private app storage. It is not sent to us and we cannot read it:

  • Your saved library and the reading position within each book
  • Books you download, stored as ordinary files in the app's own folder
  • App settings, such as your theme and reading preferences
  • Counters used to decide when to show a rating prompt

Deleting the app removes all of it.

3. Information sent to others

WhoWhat is sentWhy
Project Gutenberg, via the Gutendex catalogue API Your search terms, and your IP address as part of any web request To return matching books and their cover images
Google AdMob Advertising identifiers, device and app information, coarse location inferred from IP, and ad interactions To select, deliver and measure advertisements

We do not sell your data, and we do not operate any analytics or crash reporting service of our own.

4. Advertising

LibG is free and supported by advertising delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob and its mediation partners may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads, and in some cases to personalise them.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are shown a consent form before any personalised advertising is enabled, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can decline, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings.
  • On iOS, the system asks for your permission before the app may access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Declining is fully supported — you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Permissions

LibG requests storage access only when you download a book, so the file can be saved and reopened. It does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

6. Children

LibG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, use the Google links above or your device's advertising settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
