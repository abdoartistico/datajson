# Privacy Policy — Morphe: Video Browser

**Effective date: July 7, 2026**

Morphe ("the app", "we") is a video browser for iOS. This policy explains what data is handled when you use the app, and by whom.

## The short version

- We do **not** require an account and we do **not** collect, store, or sell your personal information on our own servers.
- Advertising and subscriptions are handled by well-known third-party services (Google AdMob and RevenueCat), which process limited device data as described below.
- Your browsing inside the app happens directly with the websites you visit (for example YouTube), under their own privacy policies.

## Data handled by third-party services

### Google AdMob (advertising)
The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process:
- Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, where you have given permission)
- Approximate device information (model, OS version, language, coarse location derived from IP)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

On first launch you are asked for consent (App Tracking Transparency and, in the EEA/UK, the Google consent form). If you decline, ads are still shown but are non-personalized.
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — Ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### RevenueCat (subscriptions)
If you purchase the Premium subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives:
- An anonymous app user identifier (random, generated on device)
- Apple purchase receipt data (product, price, purchase and expiry dates)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details.
RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Apple (payments)
All payments are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment information.

## Browsing data

Web pages you open in the app (YouTube, YouTube Music, and any other sites) are loaded directly from those services and are governed by their own privacy policies. Cookies, cache, and site data are stored **only on your device**, and you can delete them at any time from **Settings → Clear Browsing Data** inside the app.

We do not track, log, or transmit your browsing history.

## Remote configuration

At launch, the app downloads a small configuration file (feature flags and ad settings) from a static file host. This request does not include any personal data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- **Ad tracking**: You can withdraw tracking permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Browsing data**: Clear it anytime in the app's Settings.
- **Subscription**: Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact us through the support channel listed on the app's App Store page.
